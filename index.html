Put the league on your home screen

It opens full-screen, just like an app.

  1. Open the site in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (bottom of the screen, or top-right on iPad).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add. Look for the sheriff’s badge on your home screen 🤠
  1. Open the site in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu (top-right).
  3. Tap Add to home screen or Install app.
  4. Tap Install / Add. The sheriff’s badge appears on your home screen 🤠
